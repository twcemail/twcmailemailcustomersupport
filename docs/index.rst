When Should You Contact Customer Support?
=========================================

Customer support can assist with a wide range of email-related issues.

Common reasons to seek support include:

* Unable to log in to your TWC Mail account.
* Forgotten or incorrect password.
* Password reset assistance.
* Email account locked.
* Inbox not loading.
* Unable to send emails.
* Unable to receive emails.
* Email synchronization issues.
* Account security concerns.
* Email setup and configuration problems.

If basic troubleshooting does not resolve the issue, customer support may provide additional guidance.

How to Contact TWC Mail Customer Support
========================================

Support for legacy TWC Mail accounts is now provided through Spectrum. Eligible users can contact Spectrum's customer support through its official support channels, including phone support, online chat, and self-service help resources.

Before contacting customer support, gather the following information:

* Your TWC Mail email address.
* Your account username.
* A detailed description of the issue.
* Any error messages displayed.
* The device you are using.
* The browser or email application involved.

Providing accurate information helps the support team diagnose and resolve your issue more efficiently.

Prepare Before Contacting Support
=================================

Before reaching out to customer support, try these basic troubleshooting steps:

* Verify your email address.
* Check your password carefully.
* Restart your browser.
* Restart your computer or mobile device.
* Clear your browser cache and cookies.
* Confirm your internet connection.
* Try another supported browser.
* Check for temporary service interruptions.

These simple steps often resolve common email issues without additional assistance.
